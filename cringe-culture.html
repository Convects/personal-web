cringe culture

essay august 2026

this has probably been said many times over, but i wanted to put in my two cents.

cringe culture is like fashion: it's a cycle. once something gets too popular, it gets labeled as cringe, and people stop wanting to be associated with it, which makes it less popular and therefore less cringe.

i think cringe is a mix of social norms, second-hand embarrassment, and envy. why do people find kids or kids' content cringe nowadays? it's because they are envious of childhood. adults are taught that certain behaviors are okay while others aren't, which slowly evolves into cringeness.

we should be more whimsical about our lives, not take things too seriously. and following cringe culture is stopping you from doing so.
