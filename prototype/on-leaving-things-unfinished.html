on leaving things unfinished

essay march 2026

i have a folder of about forty repositories and maybe six of them run. for a long time i read that as a character defect, the kind of thing you would confess in an interview and then promise to work on.

i have stopped reading it that way. most of those forty were questions, not products. i wanted to know how a parser recovers from a syntax error, or what a route planner does when you tell it to avoid highways, and the fastest way to find out was to build the smallest thing that would answer it. once it answered, the building was done.

the trap is confusing two different activities that look identical from outside. one is making a thing for other people to use, which is not finished until it handles the case you did not think of. the other is making a thing to understand something, which is finished the moment you understand it. only the first kind owes anyone a version 1.0.

what i try to do now is decide which one i am doing before i start, and write it down. it is a small ritual and it saves a disproportionate amount of guilt later.

the six that run are the ones where i wrote down “for other people.” that is not a coincidence.
